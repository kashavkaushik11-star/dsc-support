Remote support

If troubleshooting requires hands-on help, review the Remote Support process and safety rules first. The scope and any charges must be agreed before starting. You must approve the connection and remain in control of your computer. Never share your DSC PIN, OTP, private key, password, or unattended-access code; enter any PIN yourself.

Protect your information

Do not send your token PIN, OTP, private key, account password, certificate files, or unredacted identity information. If you share a screenshot, hide names, PAN, certificate serial numbers, and other sensitive data.

Important scope note

This is independent technical guidance, not official CA or government support. For certificate issuance/renewal, account access, filing deadlines, or portal-specific decisions, contact the relevant CA or official portal helpdesk.
