DSC Renewal Process: General Checklist

A general checklist for renewing a Digital Signature Certificate through the issuing Certifying Authority, including validity checks and safe post-renewal setup.

Security first: Use official CA, token manufacturer, or portal instructions. Never share your DSC PIN, OTP, private key, or account password.

DSC renewal is handled by the issuing Certifying Authority (CA), and exact steps depend on certificate type, identity verification rules, and current provider policy. This page is a general checklist, not an application or guarantee of renewal.

1. Check the current certificate

Use the approved token manager or certificate viewer to check the certificate's validity end date and certificate type. Start early enough to allow for the CA's verification process. If the certificate is revoked or details are incorrect, ask the CA what process applies.

2. Use the issuing CA's official website

  1. Identify the CA that issued the existing certificate.
  2. Open its official renewal page or contact its published support channel.
  3. Review current identity verification, documentation, fee, and token requirements.
  4. Confirm whether your existing token can be reused or a new token is required.

3. Complete verification carefully

Follow the CA's own process and provide information only through its verified official channel. Do not send identity documents, OTPs, PINs, or payment details to an unverified agent or unofficial link.

4. Install or access the renewed certificate

After the CA confirms issuance, follow its instructions for accessing the certificate on the authorized token. Confirm the new validity dates in the official utility. Do not assume renewal is complete merely because a payment was made or an application was submitted.

5. Update portal registration if needed

Some portals may require you to register or map the renewed certificate again. Use the portal's current official instructions and verify the certificate identity before confirming.

Keep control of signing: Do not share your token PIN or allow another person to sign documents without your explicit review and authorization.

Frequently asked questions

Can DSC Support Desk renew my certificate?

This website provides independent technical guidance. Renewal must be completed through the issuing CA's official process.

Can I renew after expiry?

Ask your CA about the current process; requirements and verification steps depend on the provider and certificate type.

Need help with your exact issue?

Share the token model, Windows version, portal name, and exact error text. Hide personal details in screenshots. Never send your PIN, OTP, private key, or certificate files.

Contact DSC Support Desk on WhatsApp

DSC Support Desk provides independent technical guidance. It is not a Certifying Authority, government department, or official portal. Certificate issuance, renewal, revocation, and portal-specific decisions remain with the relevant provider.

Remote Support Available
