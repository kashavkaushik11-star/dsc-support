DSC troubleshooting guide

DSC Certificate Not Showing in Browser

Troubleshoot why a digital signature certificate does not appear in a browser or signing utility, using safe checks for token, certificate store, and portal requirements.

Security first: Use official CA, token manufacturer, or portal instructions. Never share your DSC PIN, OTP, private key, or account password.

If your DSC certificate is not listed in a browser, signing utility, or portal, first determine where it is missing. The token may not be detected, the certificate may not be available in the token manager, or the portal may be using a different certificate selection flow.

1. Check the token and token manager

  1. Connect the token directly to a working USB port.
  2. Open the token manufacturer's official management utility, if provided, and check whether the token is listed.
  3. If the token itself is missing, follow the token-not-detected guide.

2. Confirm that the certificate is present and valid

Use the official token utility or certificate viewer recommended by your CA. Check whether the expected certificate appears and whether its validity dates have passed. Do not export or share private keys or certificate files to troubleshoot this issue.

3. Check the portal's supported workflow

  • Some portals read certificates through a local signing utility rather than directly from the browser's certificate list.
  • Install and start only the signing utility specified by the official portal instructions.
  • Confirm supported browser versions and any required local service or extension.
  • Close and reopen the browser after making a supported configuration change.

4. Avoid risky fixes

Do not install random certificate bundles, disable browser security, or change system certificate stores without official instructions. If a portal shows a trust, revocation, or validity warning, verify it with your CA or portal helpdesk rather than bypassing the warning.

What information to collect

Note your Windows version, token model, browser version, portal name, whether the token appears in Device Manager, and the exact error. Hide your name, PAN, certificate serial number, and other personal details from screenshots.

Frequently asked questions

Does the certificate always appear in Chrome's certificate list?

No. The workflow depends on the token software and the portal's signing method.

Will reinstalling Chrome fix it?

Not necessarily. Check token detection, certificate validity, and the required signing utility first.

Need help with your exact issue?

Share the token model, Windows version, portal name, and exact error text. Hide personal details in screenshots. Never send your PIN, OTP, private key, or certificate files.
