Privacy Policy

Read how DSC Support Desk handles information when you visit this website or contact us.

This Privacy Policy describes, in general terms, how DSC Support Desk handles information on this informational website. It should be reviewed and updated if the site's technology, analytics, contact methods, or data practices change.

Information you provide

If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, you choose what information to share in that conversation. Please do not send DSC PINs, OTPs, private keys, account passwords, or unnecessary identity/certificate information. WhatsApp and telephone services are operated by third parties and have their own privacy policies.

Website analytics and technical data

The website may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage, such as page visits and general interaction patterns. Analytics providers may use cookies or similar technologies and receive technical information according to their own terms and settings. You can review Google's privacy information and manage browser cookie settings if you wish.

How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries you initiate.
  • To understand which guidance pages are useful and improve the website.
  • To protect the website and troubleshoot technical problems.

Sharing and retention

We do not ask visitors to provide secret signing credentials. Information you choose to send through third-party communication services is subject to those services' policies. We do not promise a specific retention period for external messages; avoid sending information that is not necessary to your enquiry.

External links

This website may link to official CA, government portal, software provider, or third-party websites. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Check their policies before sharing information or downloading software.

Children and policy updates

This website is intended for general technical guidance, not for collecting children's personal information. This policy may be revised as site features change; check this page for the current version.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us through the Contact page. Do not include passwords, PINs, OTPs, or private keys in your message.
