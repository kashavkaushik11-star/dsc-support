Certificate Not Showing in USB Token: Troubleshooting

Check why a digital signature certificate is missing from a USB token manager and learn when to contact the issuing CA.

Security first: Use official CA, token manufacturer, or portal instructions. Never share your DSC PIN, OTP, private key, or account password.

If your USB token is detected but the certificate is not visible, the issue may be that the certificate was never issued/imported to that token, the wrong token is connected, the token manager is not the correct one, or the certificate has been removed or expired. The exact cause depends on your provider and token.

1. Confirm you have the correct token

Check the token brand/model and any provider documentation. If you manage multiple certificates or tokens, verify you connected the one associated with the certificate you expect.

2. Open the official token manager

Use the management utility supplied by the token manufacturer or CA. Check whether the token is listed and whether the expected certificate appears. If Windows itself does not detect the token, start with the USB detection guide.

3. Check issuance and validity status

Contact the issuing CA through its official support channel to confirm that the certificate was issued and remains valid. A certificate can be expired, revoked, not yet issued, or associated with a different token. Do not try to copy private-key material from another computer or token.

4. Avoid initializing or formatting the token

Important: Do not initialize, format, or reset the token to try to make a certificate appear. Such actions may erase token contents or make recovery more difficult.

5. If the certificate was recently renewed

Follow the CA's instructions for certificate download/installation and confirm whether the new certificate is intended for the same token. If the portal still displays an old certificate, its registration may need to be updated according to that portal's official process.

Information to give the CA

Provide the token model, approximate issuance/renewal date, token manager status, and exact error. Never send your PIN, OTP, private key, or unredacted personal/certificate information.

Frequently asked questions

Should I format the USB token?

No. Do not initialize or reset it unless the official provider explicitly instructs you and explains the consequences.

Can a website install a missing certificate automatically?

Certificate issuance and token installation follow the CA's process. Use only the CA's official instructions.

Need help with your exact issue?

Share the token model, Windows version, portal name, and exact error text. Hide personal details in screenshots. Never send your PIN, OTP, private key, or certificate files.
