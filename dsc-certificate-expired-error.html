How to Fix a DSC Certificate Expired Error

Understand the DSC certificate expired message, check certificate validity safely, and learn when to contact your Certifying Authority for renewal.

Security first: Download software only from the token manufacturer, your Certifying Authority (CA), or the official portal. Never share your DSC PIN, OTP, private key, or account password.

A “certificate expired” message usually means the certificate's validity period has ended according to the system checking it. Installing a USB token driver again will not extend the certificate's validity. Confirm the certificate details and use your Certifying Authority's official renewal process when needed.

1. Confirm the certificate validity dates

  1. Use the token manager or certificate viewer recommended by your CA to inspect the certificate.
  2. Check the “Valid from” and “Valid to” dates. Make sure the PC's date, time, and time zone are correct.
  3. If multiple certificates appear, confirm which certificate the portal is trying to use. Do not share certificate serial numbers or personal details publicly.

2. Check whether the message really means expiry

Read the full error message. A portal can also report a certificate as invalid because it is revoked, not yet valid, not trusted, or not the certificate registered to that account. If the certificate is still within its validity dates, contact the CA or portal helpdesk with the exact error text.

3. If the certificate has expired

  1. Contact the CA that issued the certificate or open its official renewal page.
  2. Follow its current identity verification, documentation, payment, and renewal steps.
  3. Ask whether the existing token can be reused or whether a new token/certificate is required for your case.
  4. After issuance, follow the CA's official instructions to install or access the renewed certificate and update registration on any portal where required.
Important: A driver reinstall, browser reset, or changing the computer clock cannot renew an expired certificate. Never try to bypass certificate validation or use another person's DSC.

4. After renewal, verify the setup

  • Check that the renewed certificate is visible in the approved token manager or certificate store.
  • Confirm its validity dates and that you selected the intended certificate.
  • Check the target portal's instructions for updating or re-registering the certificate.
  • Test only within your authorized account and review every document before signing.

When to contact support

Contact your CA if the certificate is expired, revoked, missing, or its validity details look incorrect. Contact the target portal's helpdesk if the certificate appears valid but the portal still rejects it. Share the exact error text and general setup details, but never disclose your PIN, OTP, private key, or account password.

Frequently asked questions

Can a driver update fix an expired DSC certificate?

No. The driver enables communication with the token; it does not change certificate validity.

Can I renew a DSC on this website?

This site offers independent technical guidance only. Certificate renewal must be completed through the issuing CA's official process.

What if my certificate is valid but the portal says expired?

Check the PC date/time and which certificate the portal selected, then contact the CA or portal helpdesk with the exact error message.

Need help with your exact issue?

Share the token brand/model, Windows version, portal name, and exact error text. Hide personal details from screenshots. Never send your PIN, OTP, private key, or certificate files.
